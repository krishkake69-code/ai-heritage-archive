# National Demonstration Seed Policy

The national expansion uses **orientation cards**, not fabricated field testimony. Each generated State or region card is visibly marked as a seeded demonstration, carries an uncertainty statement, and must be replaced by a consented primary source before it is presented as a field-verified record or linked to a real practitioner.

The initial reference shortlist is grounded in the Ministry of Culture's introduction to intangible cultural heritage and the Sangeet Natak Akademi's national ICH inventory. These sources establish the archive's national reference frame, while the Indian Culture portal is used as a discovery gateway for future source enrichment.

| Reference | Use in the archive |
| --- | --- |
| [Ministry of Culture — Intangible Cultural Heritage](https://culture.gov.in/intangible-cultural-heritage) | National safeguarding context and selected Indian ICH references. |
| [Sangeet Natak Akademi — Intangible Cultural Heritage of India](https://sangeetnatak.gov.in/sections/ICH) | National inventory cross-reference for named examples where available. |
| [Indian Culture portal](https://www.indianculture.gov.in/) | Future repository discovery and source-link enrichment. |

> A State or region card communicates **coverage intent**, not ownership, authenticity, consent, or a definitive account of a living practice. Publication of a primary heritage record still requires consent, source evidence, and human review.
